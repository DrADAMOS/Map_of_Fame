Map of Fame — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Ninurta Tech ("we", "our", or "us") developed and publishes Map of Fame (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Map of Fame.

1. Information We Collect

Map of Fame does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal information in order to use the core game.

The application may store gameplay-related information locally on your device.

We do not intentionally sell personal information collected through the application.

2. Advertising

Map of Fame uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising-related identifiers, diagnostic information, and information about interactions with advertisements in accordance with Google's policies and applicable law.

For more information about how Google handles data in advertising services, please review Google's privacy information:

Google Privacy Policy

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Map of Fame uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide users with privacy and consent choices before personalized advertising is requested.

4. Map Services

Some features of Map of Fame use online map resources and related third-party services. When your device requests online resources, technical information such as an IP address may be processed by the relevant service provider.

For information about Carto's privacy practices, please visit:

Carto Privacy Policy

5. Wikipedia

Map of Fame may provide links to Wikipedia pages for additional historical and educational information.

When you open an external Wikipedia link, you are accessing a third-party service subject to its own policies.

Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The application may store gameplay progress, preferences, achievements, or other information required for the application's functionality locally on your device.

This information is not intentionally transmitted to Ninurta Tech as a personal user profile.

7. Children's Privacy

Map of Fame is a general educational and entertainment application. It does not knowingly require children to provide personal information or create an account.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions will be published at this same URL.
